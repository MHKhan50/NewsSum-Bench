NewsSum V2: TextRank vs a fine-tuned BART

Paste a news article. Two summaries are made in your browser: TextRank, which picks central sentences and needs no training, and BART-base fine-tuned on BBC News Summary. On that benchmark TextRank scored higher than every fine-tuned model, because the reference summaries are sentences copied from the article. Nothing you paste leaves your device.

The BART model (about 150 MB) downloads the first time you press Summarise.

TextRank, length-matched

Extractive · no training · length = 0.455 × article words

BART-base, fine-tuned

Abstractive model · runs locally with Transformers.js (8-bit, greedy decoding)
